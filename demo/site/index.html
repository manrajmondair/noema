Benchmark scores establish reconstruction and decoding performance. They do not establish cross-region forecasting. Each study below retains its own cohort, controls, uncertainty, and decision.

No selected animal or success trace is shown. The interactive mechanism above is synthetic; the studies here are aggregate evidence from real recordings.

What this instrument shows

This is a synthetic mechanism demonstration, not an experimental brain recording or a cross-region result. Its computed signal path is action → latent dynamics → predicted firing rates → decoded movement. The browser executes the exported model directly; its rollout is checked numerically against the Python implementation.
