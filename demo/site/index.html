A neural world model, with its limits visible

Two public benchmark results, an interactive mechanism you can steer, and three reviewed studies that failed their own prespecified criteria. Each is reported with the boundary of what it establishes.

What these do not establish

Both numbers say the model reconstructs and decodes well inside the distribution it was fitted on. Neither says it can forecast a region it has never seen. That is a separate claim, and §2 is where we tested it.

2 Reviewed evidence

Reviewed evidence

Separate claims, separate tests. Benchmark scores establish reconstruction and behaviour decoding on public splits. They do not establish cross-region forecasting, so each study below keeps its own cohort, controls, uncertainty and decision, and none of them borrows support from the benchmarks above. None of the three supported the hypothesis it was designed to test. The intervals are shown at full width because they are the finding.

3 Method

Method

The instrument in §1 is a synthetic mechanism demonstration, not an experimental recording and not a cross-region result. Its computed signal path is action → latent dynamics → predicted firing rates → decoded movement. The browser executes the exported model directly, and its rollout is checked numerically against the Python implementation.
